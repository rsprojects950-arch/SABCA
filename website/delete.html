SABCA Account Deletion Guide

Interactive slideshow showing the automated in-app deletion and administrative deletion flow for the SABCA mobile app.

Method 1: In-App (Step 1 of 3)

Profile Settings

To automatically delete your account, open the SABCA app, navigate to your main Profile screen, and scroll down to the Settings menu.

Click to Enlarge
Method 1: In-App (Step 2 of 3)

Delete Account Option

Select the "Delete Account" option. A confirmation dialog box will appear warning you about the deletion. Press "Delete Account" to confirm.

Click to Enlarge
Method 1: In-App (Step 3 of 3)

Deletion Success

Your account and associated data are immediately removed from the system. A success message is shown, confirming the action.

Method 2: Admin (Step 1 of 7)

User Requests Deletion

The user drafts an email from their registered email account detailing their request for account and data deletion, and sends it to statecoordinator@sabca.co.in.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 2 of 7)

Admin Dashboard Entry

The SABCA Administrator logs into the official admin panel. The admin console serves as the control center for all contractor profile requests.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 3 of 7)

Navigating to Manage Users

From the administrative sidebar panel, the administrator selects the "Manage Users" option to view active contractor records.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 4 of 7)

Locating the Requesting User

The administrator checks the necessities (like active dues, pending event registrations) and reviews the list of registered users.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 5 of 7)

Initiating User Deletion

The admin finds the user requesting deletion and triggers the account delete option.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 6 of 7)

Confirm Deletion

The admin console displays a verification alert warning that this action is irreversible. The administrator confirms and approves the deletion request.

Click to Enlarge
Method 2: Admin (Step 7 of 7)

Deletion Completed

The system completes the request and removes the user's data permanently. A confirmation notice is shown, and the user's access is immediately revoked.
