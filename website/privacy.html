Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 17, 2026

All users must read and understand this Privacy Policy as it has been formulated to safeguard the user’s privacy. This Privacy Policy also outlines the ways users can ensure protection of their personal identifiable information.

You must accept the contents of this Policy in order to use or continue using our website/App. This Privacy Policy detailed herein is also applicable to users of the site or mobile application through mobile or any other similar device.

The Privacy Policy of www.sabca.co.in (hereinafter referred to as "site/App") detailed herein below governs the collection, possession, storage, handling, and dealing of personal identifiable information/data and sensitive personal data (hereinafter collectively referred to as "information") of the users of the site/App.

1. Collection of Information

We confirm that we collect information from you that is required to extend the services available on the website/App.

At the time of signing up and registration with the site/App, we collect user information including name, company name, email address, phone/mobile number, postal address, and other business information which may also include business statutory details and tax registration numbers.

In this regard, we may also record conversations and archive correspondence between users and representatives of the site/App (including additional information, if any) in relation to services for quality control or training purposes.

In case of paid packages, we may collect personal information of a more sensitive nature which includes bank account numbers and related details to facilitate the sale or purchase of services available on the site/App.

We also gather and store user usage statistics such as IP addresses, pages viewed, user behaviour patterns, number of sessions, unique visitors, browsing activities, browser software, and operating systems for analysis, which helps us provide an improved experience and value-added services.

Once a user registers, the user is no longer anonymous to us and thus all information provided by you shall be stored and possessed in order to provide requested services and as required for compliance with statutory requirements.

Account & Data Deletion: Users may permanently delete their account and all associated personal data at any time directly within the App by navigating to Profile → Settings → Delete Account (or during initial onboarding). Account and personal data deletion is executed immediately upon confirmation. Users may also request administrative deletion by contacting statecoordinator@sabca.co.in.

2. Purpose and Usage of Information

The following are the purposes of collecting the Information:

  • For the verification of your identity, eligibility, registration, and to provide customized services.
  • For facilitating the services offered and available on the site/App.
  • For advertising, marketing, displaying & publication.
  • For enabling communication with the users of the site/App, so that users may fetch maximum business opportunities.
  • For generating business enquiries and trade leads.
  • For sending communications, notifications, newsletters, and customized mailers.

Please get in touch with us at statecoordinator@sabca.co.in in case you would like to object to any purpose of data processing. However, please note that if you object or withdraw consent to process data as above, we may discontinue providing you with services through our site/App.

3. Disclosure of Information

Information we collect from you may be disclosed and transferred to external service providers who we rely on to provide services to us or to you directly. For instance, information may be shared with:

  • Affiliated companies for better efficiency, higher relevancy, innovative business matchmaking, and personalized services.
  • Government, regulatory, or law enforcement agencies, as mandated under statutory enactments, for verification of identity, prevention, detection, and investigation of cyber incidents or offenses.
  • Service providers (including payment gateways, customer support, and cloud computing infrastructure) engaged for facilitating user service requirements.
  • Business partners for sending business offers directly to users.

In relation to such disclosures, receiving parties have consented to keep all information confidential and secure by using reasonable degrees of care, limiting access strictly to personnel with a need to know.

4. Reasonable Protection of Information

We employ commercially reasonable and industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, maintain data accuracy, and ensure proper use of information we receive.

These security measures are electronic as well as physical. We recommend users not disclose passwords or credentials to anyone, and sign out of accounts when completing sessions on shared devices.

5. Cookies

We, and third parties with whom we partner, may use cookies, pixel tags, web beacons, mobile device IDs, and similar files or technologies to collect and store information in respect to your use of the site and app.

Cookies help recognize browser software and provide personalized features and recommendations.

6. Data Collection Relating to Children

We strongly believe in protecting the privacy of children. We do not knowingly collect or maintain Personally Identifiable Information from persons under 18 years of age, and no part of our Site/App is directed to persons under 18 years of age.

If we become aware that a person submitting personal information is under 18, we will take immediate steps to delete the account and all related information. If you believe we might have information from or about a child under 18, please contact us at statecoordinator@sabca.co.in.

7. Data Transfers

User Information that we collect may be transferred to, and stored at, any of our affiliates, partners, or service providers. By submitting your personal data, you agree to such transfers. When we transfer personal information, we protect that information as described in this Privacy Policy.

8. Questions and Clarifications

Please contact us regarding any questions, clarifications, or grievances by emailing us at statecoordinator@sabca.co.in.
